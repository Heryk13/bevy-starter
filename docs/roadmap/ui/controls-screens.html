Key bindings: the Controls page

Roadmap row 1, proposed on 2026-11-18 for docs/specs/key-bindings/design.md. A browser mockup, not the game. The cards, switches, capsules and segments are the ones the Look built; what is new is the list of actions and the keycap. Click a keycap, then press a key: it listens, takes the key, and swaps when another action had it. Esc cancels. Keyboard and mouse only: gamepads were left for later by the user, so there is no gamepad column. In yellow, what was Claude's call on top of what the roadmap had agreed.

1 · Settings gains pages

A segmented control under the title, the one the language already uses. One level of pages (General, Controls, Dev) rather than a Controls page with two tabs inside it: one click reaches any of them. General is today's Settings, untouched.

2 · Controls, on 3d/player

One row per action a feature registered, in the order it registered them, laid out as the General page is: the label in its column, then the key. Each action has one key or mouse button. The list scrolls when it grows. Esc is not on the page: it is the way out of every panel and of this list's own listening, so it cannot change, and what cannot change is not shown (the user's call). Looking around is the mouse and is not listed: there is nothing to rebind about it. Hold a key down: its keycap goes down with it, so a binding can be tried before the panel closes.

4 · Listening

A click on a key turns its slot white and it waits for the next key or mouse button. Esc cancels and keeps what was there. While a slot listens, nothing else reads the keyboard: not the game, not Esc closing the panel.

8 · The keycap

The roadmap's variant 1, two nodes in Bevy: a grey skirt and the white face inside it. A key's legend is never translated: it is what is printed on the key. A mouse button is a keycap too.
