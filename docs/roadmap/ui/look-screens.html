Look: the game's screens in Liquid Glass

The look approved for the second spec of roadmap row 0 on 2026-11-17 (docs/specs/look/design.md). A browser mockup, not the game. Each screen holds what it holds today; only its face changes. Real sizes in px (cards of 340, 520 and 400, buttons of 44). Move the pointer over the buttons and the rows, click the switches and the language. In yellow, what was Claude's call rather than the roadmap's.

icons beside the words were tried and turned down; this shows them again

1 · Menu

No icons and no capsules. The words alone, greyish; under the pointer, a white bar, solid on the left and fading out to the right, and the word turns dark. The bar is 300 px wide and solid over its first 30%; the items touch, with no gap between them.
